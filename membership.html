Questions
Is LPG Body included in Essential or Signature?

No. LPG Body is included in Zoya’s Circle ($199/month) only. Essential does not include LPG Face or LPG Body; Signature includes an LPG session but not LPG Body.

What are Signature’s two visits a month?

A facial and an LPG session — or two facials.

How do I start a membership?

Text (224) 339-6688. No card needed to ask — tell us which tier suits you and we will confirm what is included before anything is charged.

What does “booking opens to you first” mean?

Members get first pick of the calendar. Zoya’s Circle members always get a same-week appointment.
