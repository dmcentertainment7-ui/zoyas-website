Common questions
Is the $30 deposit an extra charge?

No. It comes off the price of your treatment — you pay the balance at your visit. It exists so the chair stays reserved for you.

What if I need to reschedule?

Give us 24 hours and your deposit moves to the new appointment. It's only kept for no-shows and same-day cancellations.

Do I need a consultation first?

For lashes, facials and laser, no — book directly. For medical weight loss, yes: we start with a consultation and tell you honestly whether it's right for you.

Do you offer payment plans?

Yes — financing from $50 to $4,000 at checkout on any service.

How many laser sessions will I need?

Usually six to eight per area, four to six weeks apart, because hair grows in cycles.

Where do I park?

Free parking right outside Suite B.
