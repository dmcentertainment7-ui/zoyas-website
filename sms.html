The short version

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to cancel at any time. Reply HELP for help, or call (224) 339-6688.

Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of purchasing any goods or services.

1. Program description

Zoya’s Wellness Center, a trade name of TOZO LLC (“we,” “us,” “our”), operates a text messaging program for its clients. Messages are sent from our business number, (224) 339-6688, using our messaging provider.

Depending on how you opted in, you may receive:

  • Transactional messages — appointment confirmations, reminders, rescheduling and cancellation notices, deposit and payment links, and replies to questions you send us.
  • Conversational messages — direct replies from our staff when you text us first.
  • Promotional messages — offers, seasonal promotions, membership news and new service announcements. These are sent only to people who separately agreed to receive marketing texts.

2. How you opt in

We only send text messages to people who have given us prior express consent. You may opt in by:

  • Checking the SMS consent box on our booking or contact form on this website;
  • Providing your mobile number and agreeing in writing at our location;
  • Texting us first at (224) 339-6688 — this opts you in to conversational replies only; or
  • Separately checking the marketing box, which is required before we send you any promotional texts.

Consent for promotional messages is always a separate, unchecked, optional choice. We never pre-check it, and we never add you to promotional texting because you commented on a post, sent us a direct message, or booked an appointment.

By opting in you confirm that you are the subscriber of, or the customary user authorized to consent for, the mobile number you provided, and that you are 18 or older.

3. How to opt out — STOP

Reply STOP to any message from us to be removed. You may also reply STOPALL, END, QUIT, CANCEL or UNSUBSCRIBE. We will send one final message confirming that you have been removed, and then we will stop.

You can also opt out by calling (224) 339-6688, emailing zoyaswellnesscenter@gmail.com, or telling any staff member. Opting out of texts does not cancel your appointments — contact us separately for that.

4. How to get help — HELP

Reply HELP to any message and you will receive our name and contact information. You can also call (224) 339-6688 during business hours or email zoyaswellnesscenter@gmail.com.

5. Message frequency and cost

Message frequency varies and depends on your activity — appointment-related messages are sent as your appointments require, and promotional messages are typically sent no more than a few times per month.

Message and data rates may apply. These are charged by your mobile carrier, not by us. Check your mobile plan for details.

6. Carriers and delivery

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Delivery is subject to effective transmission by your mobile carrier and is outside our control. Supported carriers may change without notice.

7. Privacy of your mobile information

No mobile information will be sold, rented, or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent are not shared with any third parties for their own purposes.

Information may be shared with the vendors that operate the program on our behalf — for example our messaging provider and our booking system — strictly so that they can deliver the service to you, and under contract terms that prohibit them from using it for their own marketing.

Our full Privacy Policy explains what else we collect and why.

8. What we will not discuss by text

Text message is not a secure or confidential channel. To protect you, our staff and our automated replies will not:

  • tell you whether a treatment is safe, suitable or appropriate for you;
  • discuss pregnancy, breastfeeding, medications, allergies, medical conditions, side effects, or aftercare instructions;
  • promise a result or a number of sessions; or
  • request or accept card numbers, CVV codes, or bank details.

Those conversations happen in person, at your consultation, with the appropriate provider. Payments are taken only through a secure payment link or in person — never by text. If you receive a text claiming to be from us that asks for card details, it is not from us; please call us.

9. Automated messages and disclosure

Some of our replies are generated automatically. Automated messages identify themselves as such. You can always reach a person by replying to the message or calling (224) 339-6688.

10. Changes

We may update these SMS Terms. The effective date at the top of this page shows when it was last changed. Continuing to participate after an update means you accept the updated terms.
